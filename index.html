Profile Summary

✦ Yeoh Hui Qi · 杨蕙绮

Hui Qi is a bilingual and bicultural student who consistently scored Chinese 93–95 and English 88–91 from P3 to P5. Selected for the 3-year BiCEP (Bi-cultural Chinese Elective Programme), she explored Chinese Literature, Translation and Oratorical skills, joined the Beijing overseas immersion trip, and participated in the BiCEP Language and Cultural Camp with peers from 5 SHHK-affiliated primary schools. Academically, she is an MOE Edusave Scholar (top 10% nationally), a 3-year E2K Science Programme participant, with Science Olympiad Merit — a well-rounded profile ready to thrive in an Integrated Programme.


She is also a tri-performing-arts musician with 6 years of conservatory-level Guzheng training at NAFA School of Young Talents (2021–Present), achieving CCM Guzheng Grade 9 (the highest standard grade, jointly examined by NAFA & Beijing CCM) and SYT Theory Grade 1–4 with Distinction across 8 consecutive semesters. Proficient in Piano (ABRSM Grade 5, perfect Distinction streak) — a dual-instrument musician bridging Western classical and Chinese traditional music. In competition, she earned 4 international GOLD awards in Guzheng solo and 2nd Prize (亚军) at the Singapore Chinese Music Competition organised by the Singapore Chinese Orchestra.


Beyond solo performance, she has performed in Guzheng Ensemble at The Istana with NAFA (2024), SCMF Youth Music Camp (2025) and the SINGAPURA Concert (2025). In choral music, she contributed to the Nan Chiau Primary School Choir achieving SYF 2026 Arts Presentation Distinction, Singapore International Choral Festival Gold (2023) and Silver (2025).
